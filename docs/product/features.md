# Feature catalogue

Each row names the roadmap phase where it ships. Details: [vision.md](vision.md).

## Guest experience

### Before the wedding

| Feature | What the guest gets | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal link | One private link per household. Greeted by name, names pre-filled, plus-one only if granted. No password. | 1 |
| Event-aware invitation | Each guest sees only their events: civil ceremony, dinner, evening party, brunch. | 1 |
| Opening sequence | Full-screen opening with the couple's photos or the venue film, then the invitation. Skippable. | 1 |
| 60-second RSVP | Attendance per person and event, menu, allergies, children's meal, shuttle seat, overnight stay, song wish. Asks only what applies. | 1 |
| Edit until the deadline | Guests reopen their link to change answers, and get a summary and a calendar entry. | 1 |
| Language per guest | German (Swiss spelling), French, Italian or English, set per household. | 1 (de, en) · 2 (fr, it) |
| Travel & stay | Transport, parking, shuttle, room blocks with booking codes. Filled in by hand first. | 1 |
| Door-to-door travel | Public transport from the guest's town and the last connection home. | 3 |
| Reply by message | WhatsApp text or voice note, turned into an RSVP the guest confirms. | 3 |
| Ask anything | A concierge answers from the couple's content and escalates unknown questions. | 3 |
| Carpool board | Offer or ask for seats by region. | 4 |

## Swiss specifics (built in from day one)

- Four languages **per household**: de-CH (no "ß"), fr, it, en.
- **Multiple events** are the default: civil and religious ceremonies often fall on different days with different guests.
- Public transport, the last train home, shuttles from the nearest station.
- TWINT and bank QR codes, prices in CHF.
- **Hosting in Switzerland**, allergy notes treated as sensitive data, deletion 12 months after the wedding.
- Mountain and lake venues: cable car and boat times, weather plan B, altitude advice.
- Guests from abroad: airport travel, time zone, a regional guide in their language.

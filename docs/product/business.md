# Business: pricing, risks and metrics

All prices are hypotheses to test in Phase 0.

## Couples (one-time)

| Tier | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signature | CHF 390 | Curated design, personal links, RSVP, 2 languages, seating, photo wall, 12-month keepsake |
| Atelier | CHF 890 | Art-directed design, 4 languages, day-of mode, concierge, opening sequence, print files, named contact |
| Couture | from CHF 1,900 | Bespoke design, own domain, venue film, wedding-day support, printed keepsake book |

## Venues (yearly)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Venue Partner | CHF 1,500 for 10 weddings, then CHF 120 each | Profile, calendar, final-numbers and seating handoff; couples get Signature and can upgrade |
| Venue Showcase | CHF 2,900 | Partner, plus preview on the tour, venue film and guest enquiries |

Illustrative first full year: 40 direct couples × CHF 700, 8 venues × CHF 1,800,
and 30 venue couples upgrading × CHF 500 ≈ **CHF 57,000**.

## Risks

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Small, shrinking market (35,900 marriages in 2025, −2.3%) | Higher price per wedding, yearly venue revenue, later other events at the same venues |
| One-time customers | Venues, planners and photographers buy every season; engaged guests see the product at work |
| An incumbent copies features (Loveplanr: CHF 199, 5,000+ couples) | Venue contracts and finish are slower to copy than features |
| Done-for-you work doesn't scale | Track setup hours per site: under 2h before self-serve |
| Design quality depends on one person | A designer as partner from Phase 0, a design system from Phase 1 |
| A failure on the wedding day | Day-of pages work offline, monitoring every wedding Saturday, a person on call |
| Guest data | Legal review before the first paid wedding |
| Seasonality | Build in winter, deliver in summer, sell venue plans in autumn |

## Numbers to watch

| Number | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Guests answering without a manual chase | 85% |
| Median RSVP completion time | < 60 s |
| Setup hours per site | < 2 by Phase 3 |
| Couples choosing Atelier or above | 40% |
| Venues renewing after one season | 80% |
| Guest enquiries per wedding | 1 |
